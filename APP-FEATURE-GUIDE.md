# Lela – App Feature Guide

Open the in-app **App Guide** from the mobile hamburger menu (☰ → App Guide) or the desktop/tablet left sidebar. Each feature card has a direct Open link.

## Navigation
- Mobile: top-left ☰ opens all sections; the bottom bar contains quick links; More opens the full menu.
- Tablet/desktop: use the left sidebar.
- Home: dashboard shortcuts, daily goals, streak and revision reminder.

## Sections
| Section | Where to open | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Home dashboard | Home | Shortcuts, daily goals and progress summary |
| Daily lesson | Menu → Daily lesson | Daily words, sentences and practice routine |

## Device notes
- Android speech depends on an installed text-to-speech engine and English voice. Install/enable Google Speech Services and an English voice if speech is silent.
- Microphone use requires permission. Speech recognition may require internet and supported Android/browser services.
- Online dictionary lookup and speech recognition require a connection. Cached learning content may remain available offline after it has loaded.
- Progress is stored locally on the device. Clearing app/browser storage can remove it.
